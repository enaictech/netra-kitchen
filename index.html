Ready today · Maybe later

Everything on the left is yours to use from day one. Anything on the right, we'll add when your kitchen actually needs it.

Ready today 10 things

  • One pinned-message order page on mobile
  • Name, block, flat — saved on her phone, skipped next time
  • 6 products with quantity chips and live total
  • Packaging choice with auto cover-charge math
  • “Send to Netra” auto-fills the order in her WhatsApp
  • Open and closed window (8 AM to 1 PM cutoff)
  • Password-protected dashboard — phone or laptop
  • Today’s orders, grouped by your 4 societies
  • One-tap “forward” to a society group (no prices shown)
  • You change product prices and turn items on or off

Maybe later when you ask

  • Customer history across all days
  • Page refreshes by itself when a new order arrives
  • You add or rename societies yourself
  • Online payments — UPI, GPay
  • Weekly or monthly subscription orders
  • SMS or push reminders
Start small. Add the rest only when your business actually asks for it.
